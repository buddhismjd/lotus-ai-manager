# CB-0.9.4 — Commercial Buttons & Thematic Email Consent

## Product decision

Open URLs are not rendered inside answer text. Tours and products expose explicit URL actions that the web chat renders as buttons.

## Email consent boundary

- Tour interest: consent to receive information about new tours and journeys.
- Product interest: consent to receive information about new products.
- Psychologist-buddhologist consultation: email is used only for service information and booking details; it is not a tour or product news subscription.

The saved lead comment records the subscription topic, exact consent wording, interest category, interest title and conversation context.
